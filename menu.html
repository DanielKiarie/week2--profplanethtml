Our Menu

We serve a variety of traditional Kenyan dishes made with fresh, locally sourced ingredients. Our menu is designed to cater to all tastes and preferences, ensuring that every meal is a delightful experience.

Breakfast

Mandazi & Chai ya Maziwa
Freshly fried fluffy mandazi served with hot spiced Kenyan milk tea.
Mahamri & Kaimati
Sweet coconut mahamri and crisp glazed kaimati bites.
Mayai Pasua & Chapati
Two boiled eggs stuffed with fresh kachumbari, served with a soft layered chapati.

Lunch

Ugali Sukuma Wiki & Beef Stew
Hot white ugali served with sautéed greens and rich beef gravy.
Githeri Special
Boiled maize and beans fried with onions, tomatoes, and potatoes.
Mukimo & Fried Beef
Traditional mashed potatoes, corn, and pumpkin leaves served with tender beef stew.

Dinner

Nyama Choma & Ugali
Charcoal-grilled goat meat served with hot ugali and fresh kachumbari.
Kienyeji Chicken & Rice
Slow-cooked indigenous chicken in thick onion-tomato gravy with steamed white rice.
Wet Fry Tilapia & Ugali
Whole fried Lake Victoria tilapia simmered in garlic, tomatoes, and local spices.
